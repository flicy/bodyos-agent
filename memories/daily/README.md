# 30 天短期健康记录 / 30-Day Short-Term Health Logs

## 中文
按日期和参与者 `open_id` 保存必要的每日明细。只记录用户明确提供的信息；超过 30 天后，在生成周总结后删除或停止加载。不要在此目录保存诊断、身份证件、联系方式或与健康管理无关的敏感信息。

## English
Store only necessary daily details by date and participant `open_id`. Record only information explicitly provided by the participant. After 30 days, delete or stop loading the detail once a weekly summary exists. Never store diagnoses, identity documents, contact details, or unrelated sensitive data here.
